06 / DON’T BE THAT GUY

A little prep.
A better weekend.

Tickets ready. Phone charged.
Actually, just charge everything.

The grab-and-go

0 / 8

Your checklist saves only on this device.

Friday: dress for the whole night
E11EVEN asks for upscale nightlife attire and excludes shorts, beachwear, caps, sports jerseys, flip-flops and oversized clothing. Wear a sharp shirt, proper trousers and suitable closed shoes—or plan a change after the game. Door staff have the final say.Official dress code
Your club ticket has its own rules
Have your own ticket and physical photo ID. Check the entry cutoff on your ticket; a late DJ set does not mean you can turn up whenever. Premium tables and bottles are optional, not a crew-wide bill.E11EVEN ticket policy
Saturday: don’t miss the boat
Bring swimwear, sunscreen, water, a hat and a small bag for wet clothes. The charter is 11 AM–4 PM. The marina, required boarding time and weather decision must come from Mike and the captain. Follow crew instructions; don’t swim impaired.
Rides, headcounts & getting home
Use rideshares and keep your car group together. Confirm the meeting point and passenger count before requesting a car. Never drive after drinking. Nobody quietly disappears without messaging the boys.
Sunday weather plan
Sun plan: beach or pool plus football. Rain plan: indoor NFL, Topgolf or another indoor activity. The group makes the call that morning. Hard Rock Hollywood is the late-afternoon/evening plan; gambling is entirely personal and optional.Check the National Weather Service
